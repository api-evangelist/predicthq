The 2026 FIFA World Cup will create a large and long economic impact, with $8.1B in traveler spending projected across North American host cities from June to August. The tournament's six-week, multi-city footprint will require revenue leaders to adopt dynamic pricing, plan for international travelers, and capture demand across accommodations, dining, and transportation.
