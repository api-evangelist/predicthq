Expedia Group announced a partnership with PredictHQ, integrating its verified event signals and predictive demand intelligence directly into Partner Central, the platform lodging partners use to manage their business. The integration gives lodging partners a clearer view of rising travel demand by combining PredictHQ's event data with Expedia's marketplace.
